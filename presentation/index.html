02 · CONTROL RESEARCH

MARL / HFMARL + TORAX

Built policy-aggregation code and a TORAX cold-start study comparing shared experience with local learning.

Use cases

  • Warm-start a new device with source-device policies.
  • Test how sharing affects thermal-control learning.
  • Explore role-matched exchange for heating, fuelling and current control.

Current study: thermal policy search. Broader MARL remains a research direction.

The cold-start results are mixed; a general learning speedup has not been established.

COLD START · TORAX EXPERIMENTS

32% fewer median local shots
on SPARC-like

Six paired seeds: 2 faster · 2 tied · 2 slower

Sharing a starting policy can help a new device learn with fewer local trials; the benefit depends on the device.

Stricter analysis: selection trials count toward cost, but cannot confirm competence; two consecutive successful evaluations required. Uniform federation used 240 source-training shots, excluded from these local totals. TCV-like median worsened: 13.5 → 18.5 shots. Thermal policy search, six seeds per device; a general speedup is not established.
