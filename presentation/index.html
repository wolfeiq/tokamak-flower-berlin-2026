WHY A FEDERATED WORKSPACE

Facilities retain control over disclosure

Partners can offer approved analyses while keeping their complete experimental records under local control.

When pooling is feasible, a central agent remains a valid baseline.

Policy allowances are not differential privacy. Separate facilities and improved investigation outcomes still need validation.

02 · HFMARL / CONTROL RESEARCH

TORAX powers our control experiments

Google DeepMind's tokamak transport simulator supplies the plasma evolution used by our control and cold-start studies.

Research question: can source-device experience reduce the local trials needed by a new controller?

The saved cold-start study evaluates source handover followed by local learning. It uses policy search, not PPO or SAC.

TORAX is a 1-D core transport model. These studies do not establish disruption avoidance on a physical reactor.
